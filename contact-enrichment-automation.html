Contact enrichment automation

Growth Marketing, Upscale AI

Our sales team was manually guessing emails from LinkedIn URLs for cold outreach: slow, error-prone, and a bottleneck sitting right in the middle of the funnel. I built an automation that takes a submitted LinkedIn URL, enriches the contact through Clay, and pushes the result straight into Slack and HubSpot, turning a manual lookup into a near-instant, notification-driven flow the whole team could see and trust.

How it works

  1. Submit a LinkedIn URL via Google Sheets
  2. Clay receives it through a webhook
  3. Clay enriches the contact
  4. Clay sends a Slack message with the enriched details
  5. The info is logged to a spreadsheet
  6. Zapier syncs the new rows into HubSpot

Tools

  • Clay
  • Google Sheets
  • Slack
  • Zapier
  • HubSpot
